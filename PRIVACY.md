# Privacy Policy: Sticky Mic for Claude

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

Sticky Mic for Claude does not collect, store, transmit, or share any
data.

- It requests no browser permissions.
- It runs only on pages at `https://claude.ai/`, where it adds a mic button
  next to Send.
- It makes no network requests and contains no analytics or tracking.
- It does not read, record, or process your audio or messages. Clicking
  its mic presses claude.ai's own Dictate button. Any recording and
  transcription is done by claude.ai under Anthropic's privacy policy.

Sticky Mic for Claude is unofficial and not affiliated with Anthropic.

Questions: open an issue at
https://github.com/tashreefshareef/sticky-mic-for-claude/issues
